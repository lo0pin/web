Status: February · News Entry · News Entry · Last updated: Feb 06, 2026

This section is a curated logbook of where to go—and why you’d bother. You’ll find routes worth sailing, places worth stopping, and practical notes on what to expect once you drop the hook or step ashore. Some entries are neatly infrastructured; others are gloriously simple. All are chosen for navigational sense, atmosphere, and real-world usefulness.

Browse by category below, or wander as curiosity and weather permit. Each page gathers concise descriptions, local character, and the kind of detail that matters when daylight is fading and you’d rather not improvise.
