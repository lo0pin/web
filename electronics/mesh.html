What is Meshtastic?

Status: learning-in-public · Last updated: Jan 19, 2026

Meshtastic is an open-source mesh communication system that typically uses LoRa radios. Nodes relay messages for each other, so the network can cover more ground than a single device. This page is my practical documentation: what I bought, what worked, what didn’t, and what I learned.

Why I got into it

I like systems that keep working off-grid: sailing, electronics, and now radio. Meshtastic feels like “DIY infrastructure” — small, resilient, and surprisingly educational.
